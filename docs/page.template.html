Rules

Getting a Poon

  • A single buy worth $10 or more earns one Poon, dropped automatically within seconds. Smaller buys never count, even if they add up.
  • One Poon per wallet, ever. Buying more changes nothing. The contract enforces this too, so a retried or duplicated drop can never mint twice.
  • Tokens received by transfer don't count; only market buys do.
  • Mint is free: the drop transaction is paid by the project. After 3,333 drops the contract stops minting.

Trading

  • No hold, no lock: holders can buy and sell the token whenever they like, and it never affects their Poon.
  • Secondary sales pay a 5% creator fee to the deployer: ERC2981 plus ERC721-C with OpenSea's transfer validator, so it is enforced on OpenSea.
  • Moving a Poon between your own wallets is always free. The validator only screens marketplace transfers.
  • Verified against OpenSea's live validator on a Robinhood Chain mainnet fork.

How buys are caught

Every Pons token trades on one shared bonding-curve contract. It emits CurveBuy/CurveSell, but without the token address, so the indexer follows our token's Transfers and takes the ETH amount from the curve event in the same transaction, matched by amount. After graduation it finds the DEX pool by itself.

Traits & rarity

11 traits, 99 options. The rarity score is the sum of −10·log₂(p) over all traits. Tiers are set by score percentile over regular Poons; every special type is Legendary. Expected per 3,333: ~2,100 Common, ~860 Uncommon, ~270 Rare, ~64 Epic, ~46 Legendary (≈3 Gold).
